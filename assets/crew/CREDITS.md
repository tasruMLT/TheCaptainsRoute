# Crew portraits

Every face here is **AI-generated**. None of them is a photo of a real person; the crew names and
backgrounds in `crew.js` are invented.

- Source: SFHQ-T2I, "Synthetic Faces High Quality - Text2Image" by David Beniaguev, MIT licence
  (GitHub and Kaggle). https://github.com/SelfishGene/SFHQ-T2I-dataset
- Image generator: every picture used here was made with **FLUX.1 [schnell]** (Black Forest Labs,
  model licensed Apache 2.0). Do not add pictures made with FLUX.1 [dev] from the same set: that
  model's licence is non-commercial and unclear about using its output commercially.
- Changes: resized to 256 x 256.
- Keep this file, with the licence below, together with the pictures.
